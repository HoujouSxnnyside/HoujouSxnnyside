## 🧃 About this space

I build systems, software, and weird little things that somehow become real ✦

A mix of code, design, experiments, and ideas that refuse to stay ideas.

Some are clean.
Some are chaotic.

All of them are intentional たぶん

I don't really follow trends.

I try things, break things, learn why they broke, and occasionally rebuild them better.
Sometimes the first version was already better. That's also data.

Everything here tends to become part of something bigger — even when it starts as a tiny repository at 2 AM かわいい
